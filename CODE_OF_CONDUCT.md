# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rule is short enough to fit in one line:

**Talk to people here the way you would want to be talked to about code you
wrote at eleven at night.**

What that rules out, since it is worth being explicit: harassment, insults,
and dragging somebody's identity -- who they are, where they are from, what
they believe, how they speak the language -- into a conversation about a
diff. English is a second language for most of the people who will read
this, mine included. Nobody gets corrected on their English here.

What it does not rule out: saying a design is wrong, plainly and with the
reason. That is review, not rudeness, and a project where nobody can say it
ends up with bad code and polite threads.

If something crosses the line, mail <francesc.bagur@coffeesoftware.io>.
Reports are read by me and go no further. The answer may be a word with
somebody or it may be a ban; either way you get told what happened.
